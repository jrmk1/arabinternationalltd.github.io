Who We Are

Arab International Ltd. (AIL) is a leading manpower recruiting company based in Bangladesh. We specialize in sending laborers, students, and visitors to foreign countries like Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Dubai, Sudan, Europe, the USA, and Canada.

Our roots go back to 1993, when Shah Jamal Khandakar founded J. R. Munni Travel Agency. Today, we have evolved and been relaunched as Arab International Ltd. (AIL) to better serve our global partners and clients.
