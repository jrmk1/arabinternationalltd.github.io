Who We Are

Arab International Ltd. (AIL) is a leading manpower recruiting company based in Bangladesh. We specialize in sending laborers, students, and visitors to foreign countries like Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Dubai, Sudan, Europe, United Systems of America and Canada.

Our roots go back to 1993, when Shah Jamal Khandakar founded J. R. Munni Travel Agency. Today, we have evolved and been relaunched as Arab International Ltd. (AIL) to better serve our global partners and clients. His passion for helping people find jobs inspired him to start J. R. Munni Travel Agency in 1993 (Bangladesh). J. R. Munni Travel Agency was founded by him in 1993 in Bangladesh and later partnered with Al-Habib Recruite Agency (Hawali, Kuwait) and Suad Mawazi Mizban Office in Kuwait. The company was recently relaunched as “Arab International Ltd.”

If you have any questions or would like more information about our services, please don’t hesitate to contact us.
